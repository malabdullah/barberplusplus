# Booking update emails

Website guests enter a required email and optional GCC phone. Neither is verified; these are contact details, not authentication or ownership proof. A guest email never selects, updates or links an existing customer account. When a supplied optional phone is already used, the customer can sign in or omit it; global phone uniqueness remains in effect. Contact details remain on the canonical customers record.

Signed-in customers retain their existing saved-contact and verified-phone checkout. Emails use the verified Auth account address. A guest can explicitly attach exactly the receipt’s booking after signing in to the matching verified email; receipt possession, its one-hour deadline and the live session are independently checked. Existing account details and unrelated appointments are never merged or overwritten. Legacy native phone proofs, closure/recovery and package contracts are retained. Native UI changes require a separate handoff.

Emails cover request received (pending), confirmation, rescheduling, customer-visible service/price/barber/shop changes, and cancellation. No reminders, review requests or note-only changes are sent. Rendering supports English/Arabic with Kuwait time, shop, barber, purchased service/package snapshot, duration, price and reference. Staff cancellation reasons are included. Guest email links open booking; signed-in links open the account and require authentication.

A private, indexed outbox records committed booking revisions. Superseded, expired, paused and recipient-changed jobs are suppressed. Leases prevent concurrent claims; acknowledged jobs never requeue. Retries retain the same payload and Resend idempotency key, with at most six attempts within 23 hours (inside Resend’s 24-hour idempotency window). Email failures do not roll back a booking or alter push delivery. Email recipient/message snapshots are delivery records, not an additional editable customer profile.

New email tables use RLS. Public RPCs are service-role only; the dispatcher also checks an independent server secret and exact environment URLs. No delivery is enabled by migration and no historical notifications are replayed. Before production activation, separately provision customer_booking_email_runtime and the Vault secret through configure_customer_booking_email, deploy customer-booking-email and preserve the existing RESEND_API_KEY. Sender: Barber++ <noreply@barberplusplus.com>; replies: support@barberplusplus.com. A minute cron wakes the dispatcher. This is independent of the native APNs switch.

Hosted Test captures messages only in its private inbox, even if provider credentials happen to exist. Its separate ops/cloud-test migration adds idempotent capture and must never be included in a production release. Production remains unchanged until owner approval. Test proof requests are rate-limited by hashed email and IP; addresses are not account lookups or authorization claims.
