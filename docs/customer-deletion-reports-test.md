## Financial privacy

Deletion keeps completed sales, amounts received, refunds, payment methods,
dates, outstanding balances, expenses and correction links. Future appointments
are cancelled only with the customer's existing explicit consent. A deposit is
not automatically refunded: it remains visible as money owed to the customer.

Payment references and reasons associated with the deleted customer's bookings
are cleared in the same transaction. Existing financial export rows are redacted
without changing their frozen totals. Exact request retries remain possible via
one-way fingerprints; they do not retain the original personal text. Later
payments, refunds and corrections also redact text and keep retry protection.
Other customers, manager sessions and unrelated expenses are unaffected.

Operational report detail is anonymized and affected snapshots are invalidated
as before. Newly generated reports preserve anonymous historical sales. Downloaded
Excel files and previously delivered external copies cannot be recalled.
